Engineered for Rowing Performance

A simple, zero-fluff training companion that adjusts to your goals instantly. No accounts, no ads, just high-quality interval architecture.

Intelligent Auto-Scaling

Your session length remains locked. Add or remove intervals, and every single segment length dynamically stretches or shrinks to fill the exact target time. Stack the work, CrewCall does the math.

Coordinated Intensity Rates

Row through structured zones calibrated to keep your heart rate dialled. Low intervals set a steady base at 24 strokes, Medium intervals test you at 28 strokes, and High intervals challenge you at 30+ strokes.

Eyes-Free Audio Guidance

Focus on your stroke, not the screen. Seamless audio transitions use custom beep pitches to signal low, medium, and high intensity boundaries, along with a discrete 3-second countdown tick.

Endurance-Driven Bias

The builder applies a structured cycle bias (low → medium → high → medium) while actively preventing back-to-back repetitions. This ensures a balanced build and flush cycle for maximum cardio loading.

Surprise Me Builder

Paralyzed by decision? Hit the Surprise Me button. The engine generates a completely randomized, fully balanced sequence of interval ratios conforming to your target duration in a single click.

Pure Performance Interface

No account logs, no telemetry, no tracking. Built using modern oklch color palettes and responsive layout engineering, CrewCall loads instantly and works offline on any device. Ready when you are.

Inside the App

A unified, high-contrast dashboard designed to keep your metrics readable even at max-effort stroke rates.

Custom Session Planner

Define your target length and use intuitive counters to customize the high, medium, and low intensity stack. Your session timeline automatically stretches to fill the target time.

Dynamic Balanced Randomizer

With "Surprise Me", CrewCall instantly mixes, groups, and spaces out interval ratios. You get a balanced build-up, peak-work, and cooldown flow ready to start immediately.

High-Contrast Active Coach

Large, readable text shows your current zone, remaining interval time, target stroke rate, and session progress bar. Designed to be visible from your rowing machine seat.

Session Accomplished

Congratulations, session complete! A celebratory beep sequence plays when you finish, displaying your workout metrics. Ready for your next piece?

Ready to control the slide?

CrewCall runs entirely in your web browser. No installs or app store downloads required. Set your session and start rowing.
