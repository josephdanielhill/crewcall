Three gears

Every session is built from three intensities.

Each phase carries its own stroke-rate target, so you always know how hard to pull.

High
30 spm

Short, sharp bursts. Empty the tank, then recover. Designed for maximum cardio push.

Medium
28 spm

Sustainable working pace. The backbone of your endurance work and core session blocks.

Low
24 spm

Easy recovery strokes to catch your breath, reset posture, and roll into the next hard set.
