Privacy Policy

Last updated July 12, 2026

CrewCall is an interval rowing coach that runs entirely in your browser. This page explains what data is collected when you visit this site or use the app.

The app itself

CrewCall does not require an account, does not collect any personal information to function, and stores your workout configurations locally in your own browser. Nothing you enter into the app is sent to a server.

Analytics

This site uses Fathom Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand overall visitor traffic. Fathom is cookieless — it does not use cookies, does not store any persistent identifier in your browser, and does not track you across other websites.

Fathom's script collects aggregate, anonymous data such as:

  • Page views and referring site
  • Approximate location (country/region, derived from IP address)
  • Browser, operating system, and device type

Your IP address is used briefly to determine approximate location and is not stored. No individual visitor can be identified from this data. For more detail, see Fathom's own privacy policy.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach out via the CrewCall page.
